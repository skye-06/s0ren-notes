![图 2](../assets/计算题总结-d1a34823f546/images/figure-02.png)

![图 3](../assets/计算题总结-d1a34823f546/images/figure-03.jpg)

**这里的解答是错误的，应该如下：**

最小错误率：判决函数是$g_{1}(x) - g_{2}(x)$，决策方程是$g_{1}(x) = g_{2}(x)$

最小风险错误：判决函数是$R\left( \alpha_{1} \middle| x \right) - R\left( \alpha_{2} \middle| x \right)$,决策方程是$R\left( \alpha_{1} \middle| x \right) = R\left( \alpha_{2} \middle| x \right)$
